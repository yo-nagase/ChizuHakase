# App icon concepts

Generated as visual exploration for **わくわく ちずクイズ**. These files are
concept samples only; the shipping AppIcon asset has not been changed.

## Shared prompt

> Create a square iOS/iPadOS app icon concept for a Japanese children's
> geography quiz app. Use the existing icon as a reference for the recognizable
> Japan silhouette, warm cream background, and orange brand color. Keep the
> subject inside the app-icon safe area and readable at 60 px. Use friendly,
> polished, vector-like paper or sticker forms for children ages 5–9. No text,
> letters, numbers, watermark, transparency, or external rounded-square frame.

## Concepts

1. **Rainbow sticker** — Japan as one large die-cut sticker, with its major
   regions filled by the app's eight soft colors and a few restrained gold
   sparkles.
2. **Map quiz** — a bold orange Japan silhouette paired with one oversized teal
   question mark.
3. **Location pin** — one large teal location pin with the orange Japan
   silhouette inside its circular head.
4. **Collectible card** — a slightly tilted collectible card with a sky-blue mat,
   orange Japan, and one gold upgrade sparkle.
5. **Compass** — a playful teal explorer compass badge with Japan as its central
   directional motif; no compass letters.
6. **Map puzzle** — Japan assembled from a few large, colorful interlocking
   regional puzzle pieces.
7. **Magnifier** — an oversized teal magnifying glass discovering Japan on a
   pale-blue sea, with one gold highlighted area.
8. **Sticker album** — an open cream-and-teal sticker album with a large colorful
   Japan sticker bridging its pages and one card peeking behind.
9. **Achievement badge** — a gold mastery badge with orange Japan, a restrained
   rainbow foil ring, and short ribbon tails.
10. **Tap map** — a simplified orange Japan map with one gold area being tapped,
    shown by large teal ripple rings.
11. **Scholar cap** — a large orange Japan sticker wearing a small teal
    graduation cap, paired with one gold knowledge sparkle.
12. **Lightbulb** — an oversized discovery lightbulb containing the orange
    silhouette of Japan, with a simplified teal base.
13. **Discovery star** — a bold magnifying glass focused on one gold star over
    an orange Japan map.
14. **Pop-up atlas** — an open teal-and-cream atlas with a large Japan sticker
    rising from the center fold.
15. **Scholar seal** — a flat, friendly certification-stamp symbol with a teal
    scalloped edge, orange Japan, and one gold star.
16. **Map scholar** — the Japan sticker itself becomes the “map scholar,” using
    a small teal graduation cap and simple round glasses.
17. **Scholar glasses** — oversized round glasses with Japan in one lens and a
    gold discovery star in the other.
18. **Goal trail** — a short teal learning trail crossing Japan from Kyushu to a
    gold goal star above Hokkaido.
19. **Field notebook** — a thick teal study notebook with a large Japan sticker
    and a gold star bookmark tab.
20. **Learning cards** — three overlapping flashcards showing Japan, a gold
    sparkle, and a colorful prefecture-like puzzle piece.
21. **Complete the map** — a teal colored pencil finishing the final part of the
    orange Japan sticker with a gold completion sparkle.
22. **Spoken answer** — Japan inside a large teal speech bubble, representing
    saying and hearing prefecture answers aloud.

## Generation

Generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow. The existing app icon was used
as a shape and palette reference; concept 4 and concept 8 also used existing
specialty-card artwork as a style reference. Concepts 11–16 additionally use
the working app name **めざせ！ ちずはかせ** as the creative direction, with
learning, discovery, and becoming a map expert represented visually rather
than with text inside the icon. Concepts 17–22 broaden that direction with a
goal trail, scholar tools, study materials, map completion, and the app's spoken
answer interaction.
